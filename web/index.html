crv2svg

Open a .crv file without Vectric

Reads project files (.crv, .crv3d) saved by VCarve, Aspire or Cut2D and saves the drawing as SVG or DXF, so you can open it in the CAM program you already use.

Drop your file here or .crv or .crv3d. You can pick several at once.

Your files never leave this computer. The page sends nothing anywhere.

    Layers

      Reads lines, arcs and curves on their layers at the job's real size. Toolpaths, tools, 3D models and embedded pictures are not read. Source and the command-line version: github.com/munzzyy/crv2svg. Free software, GPL-3.0-or-later.
